# Code of Conduct

## Scope

This policy applies to the repository, its issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and any space where
someone is representing the project.

## The short version

Be direct about the work. Be decent to the person.

Technical criticism is expected and welcome. Contempt is not. The difference is whether the comment helps
someone find the flaw or only tells them they are stupid.

## Expected behavior

- Critique the artifact, the claim, or the method — with specifics and evidence.
- Assume the other person is competent until the evidence says otherwise.
- Say "I don't know" and "you're right" when they apply. Both are cheap and both save time.
- Give context when you disagree: what you expected, what you observed, why it matters.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a contribution for scope reasons without it being a judgment of you.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Personal attacks, insults, or sustained hostility.
- Harassment of any kind, including sexual attention, threats, or intimidation.
- Discriminatory comments about identity, background, or experience level.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Deliberately derailing technical threads, or repeatedly re-litigating a settled decision without new
  evidence.
- Submitting content that is copied without attribution or license compatibility.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, reject contributions, or block accounts. The
response is proportional to the behavior and to whether it is repeated.

If you experience or witness a violation, contact the maintainer privately through the profile linked on
the repository. Reports are handled confidentially. Retaliation against a reporter is itself a violation.

## On tone

This project prefers blunt technical writing: short sentences, concrete claims, no hedging for politeness.
That style is allowed and encouraged. It is not a license to be cruel, and "I'm just being direct" is not
a defense when the target was the person rather than the work.

## Attribution

This is an original, deliberately short policy. It is not the Contributor Covenant and does not claim to
be. If the project grows to the point where a formal, adopted policy is warranted, that change belongs in
a pull request with the rationale recorded.
